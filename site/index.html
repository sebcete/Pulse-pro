Beyond Congress — simulated strategies

Full hedge-fund 13F holdings are quarterly, delayed, and stored by CUSIP, not ticker — they cannot be turned into a reliable, low-maintenance daily feed here, and inventing them would be misleading. What this app does show, honestly, are simulated strategies built only from the disclosed trades above, plus the SPY benchmark. No real AI fund is tracked.

Alerts, honestly

A free GitHub Pages home-screen app cannot send true iPhone push notifications — that needs a backend and push service, which is neither free nor low-maintenance. What you get here, free: a daily snapshot (02:17 UTC workflow), a “new since your last visit” banner at the top of this page, and new filings highlighted in the trade list. Open the app once a day and you will see what changed.

How this works, and its limits

  • Returns start at the first close strictly after the filing date, to the latest available close, using adjusted closes where available.
  • “Holdings” here mean tickers that appear in disclosed buys, not confirmed current ownership. People may have sold since, and disclosures do not show exact share counts.
  • Coverage is roughly the most recent 190 days of filings, capped in the snapshot at 1,800 trades and 120 priced tickers plus SPY.
  • Sources: MacroWombat House JSON, Austin Starks Senate Parquet data, Yahoo Finance prices. Use only for lawful, personal, noncommercial research.
  • This tool is unaffiliated with Autopilot, congressional offices, or GitHub, and it is not financial advice.
